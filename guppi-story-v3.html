01 · the idea

Same engine. Different mind.

Most software is objective: same bytes, every install, no opinion. GuppiWheel is subjective — the same engine, but every instance accretes its owner's corrections, domain, and lived experience.

Objective · code
Same bytes, every clone, no memory. Fine for software. An engine update can clobber what you taught it. Knowledge lives in people's heads and leaves when they do.
One truth. No ownership. Ages out.
Subjective · the wheel
Every output is an artifact in your own Snowflake account. Corrections become permanent memory. The engine is additive-only — updates never overwrite what you taught it. The audit trail is yours, and unreproducible.
Many lived experiences. Owned. Compounds.
It isn't a repo you clone.
It's an engine that remembers.
02 · the reveal

Guppi runs on a wheel

Under the friendly name is a real engine — the GuppiWheel.

One trip around is a spin — the five stages orbiting the outcome at the hub. A loop is a spin that proved out and got packaged: the thing a customer subscribes to. Outputs are the orbit; the outcome is the point.
Stages are enforced in code, not convention — nothing advances until the gate is satisfied.

04 · newest to the wheel

From outputs to outcomes

Most systems ship outputs — a dashboard, a model, a brief — and stop. The wheel now closes the loop to the outcome: did it actually change the world?

OUTCOME is a first-class artifact type with its own lifecycle — a target is stated before the work, committed to, wired to live data, then measured against reality.

ASPIRATIONAL→ SELECTED→ TRACKED→ RESOLVED

ASPIRATIONAL the target, stated up front  ·  SELECTED a decision-maker commits  ·  TRACKED wired to a live signal (a query, a metric, a URL)  ·  RESOLVED measured against the world.

An outcome doesn't get to claim RESOLVED until its pointer resolves to real data or a human signs off. Aspiration is cheap; the wheel makes you pay it off.

05 · the doctrine

The rules are the engine

28 rules, enforced in code — not a style guide. Anyone can hire AI agents. Few adopt the operational doctrine that makes them compound without corrupting.

Foundation — how work enters the wheel

RULE-013
Headless First
Every output is a queryable artifact before any render. If it isn't in the table, it doesn't exist.
RULE-014
Status Ownership
The actor that owns a transition sets the status. Submitters initiate; agents advance; humans sign off.
RULE-017
Separation of Execution
Cowork dispatches, Rocky researches. Agents don't cross boundaries — that's how loops are prevented.
RULE-018
Launchables Live in the Wheel
Apps, dashboards, narratives carry one launch shape. Bytes live on the stage, never in a local path.

Integrity — the substrate stays true

RULE-019
Engine Additive-Only
No drops, no renames, no breaking changes. Your corrections accrete forever — "we never break your stuff," enforced by code.
RULE-025
Serving Shows Current Truth
Consumer surfaces index only non-superseded artifacts. Supersede with a pointer, never delete. History stays queryable.
RULE-026
Grounding Sweep
A doctrine change isn't done until every grounding surface is swept — rules, semantic views, search, agents, docs, memory.
RULE-029
Artifact IDs Are Unique
All creation flows through one gated write path that allocates atomically and refuses duplicates. Snowflake won't enforce it — the wheel does.
RULE-016
No Self-Spawning
No agent holds a tool that creates work for itself. If it could re-invoke itself, it would loop. So it can't.
RULE-020
Initiative-vs-Research Lineage
One goal is one initiative; iterations are research children under it. Keeps the lineage legible, not sprawled.

Distribution — how the wheel travels

RULE-021
Share Composition
Shares carry concepts, not bytes — framework by default, exemplars by explicit curation. Customer-private content never leaves.
RULE-015
Collaboration Tags
Route work to a person by tagging them; they discover it on their next session. A pull model, not a ping.

Governance — who is allowed to change what

RULE-024
Secrets Hygiene
Credentials come from strict env/SECRET, never literals. Repo-wide grep returns zero. Born from a real leak we fixed.
RULE-028
Procedure-Mediated Writes
No role below admin holds direct DML. Writes flow through owner-procs; doctrine is admin-only. The lockdown ships born-locked.
RULE-027
Doctrine Authority Is Orchestrator-Only
Sub-agents operate within doctrine: they propose via artifacts, never write rules. Only the orchestrator decides.
06 · what runs the wheel

The cast

Server-side Snowflake agents, each with one job and a hard boundary. They keep the wheel turning without a human at the crank.

Rocky
Autonomous research
Every 5 minutes, picks up a queued initiative, researches it on the open web, and writes the findings back as an artifact. Web search only — never spawns its own work.
Cowork
Dispatch & ask-the-wheel
The conversational front door. Submits initiatives, advances stages through the rules engine, and answers questions about the wheel in natural language via Cortex Analyst.
TARS
Independent audit
Scores trust on a different model than the builder — adversarial by construction. Three-vote: the builder recommends, TARS verifies, the human decides.
Stewart NEW
Grounding steward
Watches the substrate for drift — duplicates, orphans, dead references — and proposes fixes. It cannot change doctrine; it has no tool that writes rules. It even watches its own builder.

The boundary is structural, not a promise. A sub-agent can't change doctrine because it has no capability to — only the orchestrator and the human do. Propose, verify, decide.

07 · the proof

Built on itself

GuppiWheel is built using GuppiWheel. This page is an artifact. The rules were exercised on their authors first — we caught ourselves violating headless-first and shipped the recursive fix in one session.

On Stewart's very first scan, it caught a bug in the code that builds the wheel — a write the orchestrator itself had made — and filed a proposed fix. The detective caught its own creator. The boundary held: it proposed, it did not change a thing.

The proof isn't a claim. It's in the wheel, queryable, with an audit trail.

The rules are the moat — not the bots.
