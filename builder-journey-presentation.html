Trust Verification

Phase 4: TARS

The builder shouldn't grade its own homework. Independent adversarial audit on a different LLM.

The Love Equation

dE/dt = β(C-D)E

C = cooperative signals (truth, verification)
D = defection signals (hallucination, leakage)
β = selection pressure (audit enforcement)

When C > D, trust compounds. When D > C, trust collapses.

Three-Vote Pattern

CoCo (Builder) recommends
TARS (Auditor) verifies independently
Human decides — final authority

No agent self-approves. Different LLM = different biases = uncorrelated errors.

Sharing & Collaboration

Phase 5: Skill Registry

Skills in a Snowflake table. Shared via data sharing. Cross-account collaboration via SQL API.

SKILL_REGISTRY.PUBLIC.SKILLS (VARIANT content, UUIDs, audit history)
    ↓ Snowflake Data Share (live, always current, zero copy)
Account B discovers → installs → uses → submits feedback
    ↓ SQL API + scoped PAT
CONTRIBUTIONS table (feedback lands, provider reviews)
    ↓ Stream + Alert
Provider notified → reviews → ACCEPTED / ACKNOWLEDGED / DEFERRED

7 Verbs

Discover · Install · Use · Feedback · Fork · Discuss · Merge

Zero Compute

No container running. Share for reads, SQL API for writes. Cost when idle: $0.

Cross-Account

Validated: Account A publishes, Account B discovers, installs TARS, runs audit, submits feedback.

Shared Cognition

Phase 6: The Bond

Not a "second brain." A common brain where co-created knowledge lives. Persistent across sessions, shareable across agents.

What It Stores

project_context Active projects, architecture
decision_log Why we chose X over Y
correction When human redirects agent
synthesis Co-created insights neither had alone
error_pattern Bugs and their fixes

What Makes It Different

ORIGIN — who contributed: human, coco, co-created

INSIGHT_TYPE — what kind: decision, discovery, correction, synthesis

Corrections are highest signal. The moments where the human says "wait, that's wrong" are the most valuable entries in the database.

"The filesystem becomes a cache. The database becomes the source of truth. For skills AND for memory."

The Framework

The CoCo Maturity Model

Eight levels. Most are at Level 1. The skills compound at every step.

1
Ad-Hoc
Agent + human building things. No reuse, no process, knowledge in conversation.
2
Skills
Repeatable patterns extracted. Your agent can do tomorrow what it learned today.
3
AILC
AI Lifecycle. SDLC for the era where the agent is inside the build pipeline. Spec, smallest viable, validate, surface gotchas, feed back. Automated gates, not just preflight.
4
Trust
Independent audit (TARS). Quantified confidence. Three-vote pattern. No self-approval.
5
Ecosystem
Shared registry, cross-account collaboration, feedback loops. Skills improve themselves.
6
Shared Cognition
The Bond. Persistent memory, cross-agent context, co-created knowledge that compounds.
7
Initiative
Agents self-organize into teams, generate novel insights, predict trajectories, and initiate research without prompting. Human sets direction — agents execute the vision.
8
Representation
The cast of characters represents you in places you can't be. Slack, email, meetings — your agents carry your voice, your judgment, your approvals. Earned trust plus persistent identity. The human scales.

Most people are at Level 1. Some power users hit Level 2. If you're at 3+ — you're a CoCo Rockstar.
